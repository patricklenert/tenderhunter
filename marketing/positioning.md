# Positioning

## Primary Audience

Small and medium-sized German bid and business-development teams that handle
many public tenders with limited time. The first campaign speaks to the person
who has to decide which opportunities deserve a closer look before the team
spends hours on documents and internal coordination.

The U.S. opportunity workflow is a later localization. It should not be mixed
into the first German explainer.

## Core Problem

Relevant public tenders are spread across portals and documents. A team can
find many notices, but the difficult first decision is whether a tender fits its
capabilities, region, requirements, documents, and available effort.

## One-Line Positioning

Tenderhunter helps small and medium-sized companies turn public tender search
into a focused first decision: discover opportunities, structure the important
requirements, and prioritize the ones worth reviewing.

## Message Hierarchy

### 1. Outcome

Spend less time sorting irrelevant opportunities and reach the next sensible
review step faster.

### 2. Mechanism

- Tenderhunter monitors configured public sources.
- It structures tender details and document information.
- It compares the tender with a company profile.
- It surfaces fit, effort, missing requirements, deadlines, and stop signals.

### 3. Product Promise

Tenderhunter is a decision aid for the first prioritization. It does not decide
whether a company should bid and it does not replace legal, commercial, or
technical review.

### 4. Proof

Use real product screens, real source names that are currently enabled, and
specific examples of structured requirements. Do not use invented customer
results or generic AI success statistics.

### 5. Call To Action

Primary German CTA: `Website pruefen lassen`

Supporting CTA: `Kostenlose Top-3-Vorschau starten`

The second CTA must remain qualified as `bis zu drei Vorschlaege`, because the
preview can return fewer than three reliable matches.

## Tone

- Direct, calm, and practical.
- Slightly humorous when showing the problem.
- Precise when describing product behavior.
- Confident without promising a win.
- Concrete verbs before abstract AI language.

## Objections To Address

### "Wir kennen unsere Portale bereits."

The value is not only finding a notice. It is reducing the first sorting and
comparison work across notices, requirements, deadlines, and company fit.

### "Ein Score kann nicht fuer uns entscheiden."

Correct. The score is a prioritization signal, not a bid recommendation or a
guaranteed outcome.

### "Wie aktuell sind die Daten?"

Use the current source and monitoring configuration for each campaign. Never
claim daily or near-real-time freshness without a verified operational value.

### "Was passiert mit unserer Website?"

Marketing copy must describe the website preview and external processing in a
way that matches the actual privacy notice and runtime behavior.

### "Wir brauchen belastbare Unterlagen."

Show where document roles, requirements, deadlines, and missing information
are surfaced. Do not imply that every source document is always available or
complete.

## Language Guardrails

Prefer:

- `erste Priorisierung`
- `strukturierte Anforderungen`
- `passende Vorschlaege`
- `belastbarer Treffer`
- `Stopp-Signal`
- `naechster sinnvoller Pruefschritt`

Avoid:

- `alle Ausschreibungen`
- `garantiert passend`
- `automatisch gewinnen`
- `100 Prozent vollstaendig`
- `mehr Umsatz garantiert`
- `KI entscheidet fuer Sie`
